Privacy Policy

Pomodoro Timer — Last updated: July 30, 2026

Pomodoro Timer ("we", "our", or "the app") is a simple focus-timer tool. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app. By using Pomodoro Timer, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any names, email addresses, device IDs, location data, or any other personal identifiers.

The only data the app stores is your timer settings (focus duration, break duration, session count) and local usage history (number of completed sessions per day). This data never leaves your device.

2. Collection Methods

All data is stored locally on your device using the device's built-in local storage (AsyncStorage). No cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics SDKs are used. The app does not employ any automatic data-collection tools.

3. Use of Data

The locally stored data is used for one purpose only: to run the app's core functionality—displaying your timer durations, tracking your session history, and remembering your preferences between uses.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. The app has no backend server, no analytics provider, no advertising network, and no cloud storage integration. All data remains exclusively on your device.

5. User Rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is nothing to view, update, or delete from our side. You may clear the app's local data at any time through your device settings (Settings → Apps → Pomodoro Timer → Storage → Clear Data).

6. Data Security

Since no data is transmitted or stored on any server, there is no risk of server-side breach. The local data on your device is protected by your device's built-in operating system security. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

7. Children's Privacy

The app does not target children under the age of 13, and it does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us so we can investigate.

9. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Users will be notified of material changes through an in-app notice on the next launch after the policy is updated.
