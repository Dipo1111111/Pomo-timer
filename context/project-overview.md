# Project Overview — Pomodoro Timer PWA

## What
A beautiful, personal Pomodoro timer web app that works as a PWA on iOS. Runs the classic focus/break cycle with session history tracking.

## Why
The user needs a visually refined, reliable focus timer they can add to their iOS home screen. Existing Pomodoro apps are either ugly, over-engineered, or don't work offline.

## Who
- **Primary user**: The developer themselves. Personal use, single-user.
- **Environment**: Desk workstation (laptop/desktop) and phone (iOS Safari, home screen PWA).
- **Context**: Low-distraction focus sessions. The timer lives in a browser tab or as a standalone PWA.

## The Problem
Most Pomodoro timers are either:
- Functional but ugly (generic SaaS design, no personality)
- Over-engineered with accounts, sync, and teams
- Not available as a polished PWA on iOS
- Lack session history/stats

## Core Philosophy
Minimalist luxury. The timer is the hero. Everything else is secondary and accessed without breaking focus. The app feels like Apple designed a Pomodoro timer — clean, confident, precise.

## Success Metrics
- **Day 1**: Timer runs perfectly — start, pause, reset, auto-cycle through focus/break phases. Audio alert works. Looks beautiful.
- **Day 30**: Used daily. Stats show meaningful data. Installed as iOS home screen PWA.
